Overview

Dental fitouts in Melbourne, start to finish

When the time comes to construct a brand new dental office, or to renovate your existing dental office interior, BuildMarque is the team to call.

Our dental fitout specialists take care of every step of the dental clinic interior design process, from refurbishment and construction through to dental plumbing, creating a dental office that you, your staff and your patients feel welcome in.

We have long-standing relationships with qualified dental equipment suppliers and installers, building surveyors, town planners and inspectors, so every dental office fitout adheres to statutory requirements, including paediatric dental interiors and multi-surgery groups.
