BuildMarque delivers commercial fitouts in Melbourne with a focus on healthcare and corporate interiors including dental practices, medical centres, allied health clinics, and offices.

As commercial interior designers who also manage construction, BuildMarque takes a project from concept design and construction drawings through to permits and full turnkey construction, all under one team.

BuildMarque is based in Melbourne and delivers commercial fitouts in Melbourne and across regional Victoria, including centres such as Geelong. Contact us to confirm coverage for your location.

BuildMarque works almost exclusively in healthcare and allied environments. These include dental practices, medical centres, allied health clinics, and dental laboratories, along with corporate office fitouts.

This focus means clinical workflows, infection control, and equipment coordination are planned for as standard, not treated as an afterthought.

Cost depends on the scope of work (a new build, extension, or refurbishment), the number of rooms or work areas, the level of finish, and the equipment and services coordination required.

Healthcare fitouts carry additional compliance and clinical requirements that affect cost, so quotes are tailored following an initial consultation rather than set to a standard rate per square metre.

Timelines depend on project size and complexity, but most fitouts move through four stages: concept design, construction documentation and permit approval, construction, and handover.

For healthcare projects, permit and approval timeframes are often the largest variable, which is why they’re managed directly rather than left for clients to coordinate separately. A realistic timeline is provided at the initial consultation.

Yes. Building permit applications, authority approvals, construction documentation, consultant coordination, and compliance checks against Australian standards and healthcare regulations are all managed in-house, as part of the turnkey service.

Yes. Many BuildMarque projects are delivered inside live healthcare environments. Construction is staged in coordination with the clinical team, sequencing work to minimise disruption to patients and staff while the practice continues operating.

Look for a team that manages design and construction together, rather than handing a project between separate designers and builders. BuildMarque provides a single point of responsibility across concept design, permits, and construction.

Our work is backed by a zero-incident safety record and a multidisciplinary team of architects, project managers, and site managers with direct healthcare and commercial fitout experience.
