Overview

Medical interiors that make the right first impression

A good medical clinic interior design can give both new and existing patients and staff a great impression, reassuring them that they have come to the right place for their medical requirements.

BuildMarque is one of the premier medical interior design firms in Melbourne. Our designers guide you from initial concept design and floor plan optimisation to space planning and the selection of appropriate finishes. Once the final design is approved, we work with the building surveyor to obtain the building permit, and from there all the way to construction and final handover.

From physio fitouts to surgery fitouts, our healthcare interior fitout experts can refurbish your waiting and reception room, treatment rooms or the whole clinic. We take care of permits and approvals, source the best materials and liaise with all parties so the project meets every building code and OH&S requirement.
