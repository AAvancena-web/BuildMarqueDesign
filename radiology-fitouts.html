Overview

Specialist design and construction for imaging environments

Radiology fitouts require a level of technical precision, regulatory knowledge and coordination well beyond standard commercial construction. Imaging environments must support complex equipment, strict radiation protection, clinical workflows and patient comfort, all while complying with Australian healthcare standards.

BuildMarque delivers specialist imaging environments through a fully integrated design-and-construct approach, giving practices and healthcare operators a clear pathway from concept to completion.

Scope

A professionally delivered imaging environment may include

One team takes care of design, approvals and construction, so nothing falls between the gaps.

  • 01MRI, CT, X-ray, ultrasound and nuclear medicine suites
  • 02Shielded rooms with lead-lined walls, doors and glazing
  • 03Control rooms and reporting spaces
  • 04Patient preparation and recovery areas
  • 05Reception and waiting zones
  • 06Equipment and technical rooms
  • 07Mechanical, electrical and data upgrades
Why it matters

Built to perform, from day one.

Every technical, regulatory and operational detail is addressed by one accountable team.

Equipment accuracy

Room dimensions, vibration control, power stability, temperature and shielding integrity are engineered so equipment runs within manufacturer specifications.

Patient comfort

Lighting, acoustics, finishes and flow create calm, private spaces for procedures that can feel unfamiliar or stressful.

Compliance and risk

Radiation protection, safety, accessibility and infection control are embedded in design and construction, avoiding costly retrofits.

Radiation shielding

Lead-lined walls, ceilings, doors and glazing are coordinated with radiation reports and integrated cleanly into the building fabric.

Live clinical settings

Works are staged, sequenced or scheduled outside clinical hours so existing facilities keep operating safely.

Future-ready

Flexible layouts, accessible services and allowance for extra shielding make new equipment easier to introduce.
