BuildMarque delivers commercial fitouts in Melbourne with a focus on healthcare and corporate interiors including dental practices, medical centres, allied health clinics, and offices.

As commercial interior designers who also manage construction, BuildMarque takes a project from concept design and construction drawings through to permits and full turnkey construction, all under one team.

BuildMarque is based in Melbourne and delivers commercial fitouts in Melbourne and across regional Victoria, including centres such as Geelong. Contact us to confirm coverage for your location.

BuildMarque works almost exclusively in healthcare and allied environments, including dental practices, medical centres, allied health clinics and dental laboratories, along with corporate office fitouts.

This focus means clinical workflows, infection control and equipment coordination are planned for as standard, not treated as an afterthought.

Cost depends on the scope of work (a new build, extension or refurbishment), the number of rooms, the level of finish, and the equipment and services coordination required.

Healthcare fitouts carry additional compliance and clinical requirements, so quotes are tailored following an initial consultation rather than set to a standard rate per square metre.

Most fitouts move through four stages: concept design, construction documentation and permit approval, construction, and handover.

For healthcare projects, permit and approval timeframes are often the largest variable, which is why we manage them directly. A realistic timeline is provided at the initial consultation.

BuildMarque is led by a multidisciplinary team of architects, project managers, and site managers who bring deep technical knowledge and hands-on delivery experience to every project.
