Overview

Healthcare fitout services for Victorian practitioners

Healthcare fitout services are not one-size-fits-all. A physiotherapy studio, a specialist consulting suite, a pathology collection centre and a minor procedure clinic each have different room types, services and compliance needs.

BuildMarque offers a full menu of services, from feasibility and design through permits, construction and handover. Engage us for a complete new clinic or a targeted upgrade. Our in-house architects and project managers have delivered allied health, specialist, pathology and general practice projects.
